Optimus · fuel inventory management

What Optimus is

Optimus is an AI-powered system that manages your fuel inventory for you. It works out where a run-out is coming, how much to order and from which supplier, and warns you in advance when a delivery needs to change. All your team has to do is approve the recommendation.

Optimus pulls the data it needs from your systems and uses AI models to recalculate the whole picture in real time — across every depot and every fuel grade at once, factoring in demand, supplier lead times and supplier reliability.

  • AI demand forecast
  • Less working capital tied up in excess inventory
  • A week-ahead stock forecast
  • Less idle time
  • A decision across what-if scenarios in minutes

02 · The process

From the stock in the tank to the next delivery

The usual path is short: the system works out what is needed → a person approves it → the supplier delivers → the fuel is received at the depot. Click a step to see how it works.

What the system can do, and how quickly its data refreshes, depends on the data available on the client side and on what can be integrated with their internal systems.

03 · Integration

Data flows

Optimus keeps no master data of its own — it takes data from the company’s systems, computes the decision, and returns the finished result.

Out
  • Money saved. The system recalculates the decision against the scenarios you set, and counts the money frozen in excess stock.
  • Less idle time. Fewer losses from idle depots and idle transport.
  • Faster decisions. The whole process is automated — a person reviews a finished proposal instead of working through dozens of depot-and-fuel combinations by hand.
  • Better data quality. Cleaner data flows cut the risks that come with manual calculations and files.
