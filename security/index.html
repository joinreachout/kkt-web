Security & privacy

How we handle your data.

We ask senior people to share real, sensitive material — a P&L, a KPI dashboard, an operating situation. That only works if the data handling is honest and plain. Here is exactly what happens to what you share, in the same language we use everywhere else.

The advisor & dropped documents

Private by default. Not stored.

When you ask the advisor a question, or drop a document for it to audit, the content is processed in real time to generate the answer and then it is gone. There is no account, no database, and no log of what you typed or uploaded.

  • Zero data retention at the AI layer. Requests to our AI provider (Anthropic’s Claude) carry a zero-data-retention flag, so your inputs are not retained by the provider or used to train models.
  • No storage on our side. We run no database and write no message content to disk. A dropped file is read once to extract its text, passed to the model, and not kept.
  • No content in our logs. The only operational record is a rate-limit counter keyed to a one-way hash of your IP address with timestamps — never the message or the document.
  • You choose what to share. The advisor never requires a document. Share only what you are comfortable sharing; redact first if you prefer.

If you would not put it in an email to us, do not put it in the advisor. We treat both with the same care — but plain honesty beats a false promise.

The Cost of Doing Nothing calculator

Your numbers stay in your browser.

The calculator runs entirely on your device. The figures you enter are not sent to us — there is no submission step that transmits them — unless you explicitly choose to attach your results when booking a diagnostic. That is stated in the tool itself, and it is the whole point: it should be useful to a CFO who does not want their numbers leaving their machine.

Where we are, which law applies

An EU firm, under GDPR.

KKT is Kitty Kat Technologies OÜ , registered at Jõe 7, 10151 Tallinn, Estonia. As an EU entity we operate under the General Data Protection Regulation. Any personal data you send us directly — an email, a contact-form message — is used only to respond to you and to run the engagement, never sold and never shared beyond what a specific engagement requires.

What we do not do

The short list.

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use what you share with the advisor to train models.
  • We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking on this site.
  • We do not require an account to use anything here.
Procurement & due diligence

Need more for a review?

If your procurement or security team needs a data-processing agreement, a sub-processor list, or answers to a vendor-security questionnaire before an engagement, write to us and we will work through it directly. We would rather have that conversation early than paper over it.
