# Retail AI Canvas — full catalog (retail.kittykat.tech)

This is the complete retail offering so you can answer card-level retail
questions directly. The canvas itself still has its own advisor; you may either
answer from here or route deep browsers to **retail.kittykat.tech**.

**Shape:** 46 solution "services" (cards) across 12 domains, organized in 3 stages,
with a 5-step maturity roadmap. Start from business pressure, recommend the
smallest credible start, prefer visibility & control before optimization, treat
expansion (Stage 3) as optional and maturity-dependent.

Each card has metadata: speed (Quick Start / Scoped Project / Strategic Build),
data requirement (Light / Moderate / High), cost ($–$$$$), and impact tags
(Margin & Cash, Customer Growth, Control & Visibility, Productivity, Decision
Speed, Risk & Loss Reduction, New Revenue, Strategic Expansion, etc.).

## Stage 1 — Strengthen Core Operations

**Data Foundation** — the trusted base every retail decision depends on.
- One Trusted Retail Data Foundation — connect POS, ERP, loyalty, stock, supplier, finance & master data into one trusted base.
- One Version of Key Numbers — define key metrics once, assign business owners.
- Data Input and Document Processing — turn supplier files, invoices, contracts, forms, emails, photos, spreadsheets into validated data.

**Stock & Availability** — stop carrying the wrong stock while losing sales on the right products.
- Demand Forecasting and Event Planning — forecast by SKU/store/period with seasons, promos, events, local shifts.
- Replenishment and Availability Protection — better ordering logic, early stockout-risk detection.
- Inventory Health and Cash Release — find overstock, slow movers, aging stock, frozen cash before markdowns/write-offs.
- Freshness and Waste Control — track shelf-life risk & slow sell-through to guide markdowns/transfers/order changes.

**Warehouse & Transport Operations** — make execution visible before depot/delivery problems hit stores.
- Warehouse Stock Accuracy and Flow Control — receiving/put-away/pick accuracy.
- Picking, Loading and Shift Productivity — expose lost warehouse labor (flow, slotting, exceptions).
- Transport Planning and Delivery Control — TMS-style planning, route quality, OTIF, transport cost.

**Store Execution** — make the store network closer to the plan.
- Store Execution Control — network-wide view of where stores drift from standards.
- Field Tasks and Issue Escalation — owned workflows with deadlines, evidence, escalation, closure.
- Shelf, Price Label and Planogram Checks — detect gaps/deviations/label issues (computer vision + sales anomaly).
- Labor Planning and Productivity — align staffing to traffic, transactions, deliveries, task pressure.

**Margin & Cash** — a clearer forward view of where profit and cash move.
- Margin Visibility and Profitability Analytics — where margin is made/lost across stores, categories, suppliers, campaigns.
- Rolling Financial Forecasting — rolling view of where profit and cash head.
- Decision Scenarios Before Committing — compare financial consequences of major choices before committing.
- Working Capital and Cost Efficiency — where cash/cost is absorbed (stock, payments, labor, energy, waste, logistics).

**Supplier Performance** — make supplier performance/claims/value visible before problems hit.
- Supplier Performance and Inbound Risk — supplier reliability view, inbound risk detection.
- Claims, Disputes and Value Recovery — structured recovery of what's owed.
- Supplier Funding and Joint Planning — track agreed support, segment key relationships, focus joint planning.

**Reporting & Decisions** — turn trusted data into faster decisions.
- Management Reports and Performance Views — one trusted KPI view per level, automated packs, benchmarking.
- Alerts and Plain-Language Answers — surface action-worthy exceptions; ask business questions without an analyst.

**Loss Prevention** — detect leakage earlier, focus investigation where value is at risk.
- Checkout Fraud and Transaction Abuse — refunds, voids, discounts, loyalty, cashier & self-checkout patterns.
- Shrinkage and Stock Loss Control — unusual stock adjustments, unexplained losses, shrinkage patterns.

**Workforce & AI Assistants** — practical AI support for store, field, head-office, HR, legal, commercial, service teams.
- AI Office, Meeting and CRM Assistants — capture meetings, extract tasks, draft updates/CRM notes.
- Legal, Contract and Compliance Assistant — review, drafting, comparison, issue-spotting.
- HR, Recruitment and Onboarding Assistant — recruitment, onboarding, policy Q&A, training, comms.
- Internal Knowledge and Store Operations Assistant — fast answers from SOPs, policies, manuals, store procedures.
- Customer Service and Complaint Assistant — answer routine questions, draft responses, classify, escalate.
- Supplier Communication and Claims Follow-Up Agent — draft follow-ups, chase claims, request credit notes, escalate.

**Promotions & Pricing** — spend commercial money where it creates real return.

## Stage 3 — Expand and Monetize (optional, maturity-dependent)

**Expand and Monetize** — new revenue pools beyond classic product margin.
- Retail Media and Supplier-Funded Activation; Supplier Insight Products; In-Store and Digital Channel Monetization; Paid Loyalty and Member Benefits; Partner Ecosystem and Lifecycle Offers; Selective Platform and Partnership Models.

## Roadmap (maturity, not a calendar): see → control → optimize → scale → expand
- **see** — make current state visible (trusted facts, value-leakage). Don't build a broad platform before the first questions are clear.
- **control** — ownership, rules, exception response. Don't treat dashboards as value when nobody owns the action.
- **optimize** — better decisions on the controlled foundation (forecasting, segmentation, scenarios).
- **scale** — embed better decisions into recurring work; reduce manual dependence.
- **expand** — optional new value pools later. Rarely the first recommendation.

Sequencing bias: unclear numbers → see; missed execution / recurring losses / no ownership → control; better commercial decisions → optimize; embedding a routine → scale; new value pools → expand. The trusted-data foundation underpins almost everything downstream.
