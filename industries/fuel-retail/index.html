Industries — Fuel retail

For independent fuel networks.

Your network already has the data — ERP, POS, loyalty, GPS, depots, BI, spreadsheets. What is usually missing is connectedness: the network runs on people, calls, manual reconciliation and the experience of individual managers, while the data you have piled up does not yet work for your decisions.

AI agents are not installed on top of chaos. They earn their place once the data, rules, forecasts and control beneath them are built — so the path runs bottom-up, on the data you already have.

How the path runs

Bottom-up, on data you already have.

Every block of the network economy climbs the same way — start small on existing data, build the foundation as you go. You do not need to be at the top to begin.

Essential

Bring the picture together on data you already have — need, stock, supplier offers, your price against the market. Cheap, fast, no models.

Engagement starting points

Across the whole network economy.

The effect is across the whole network economy — procurement and margin, pricing, logistics, stations, finance and control. We start where the diagnostic points. Optimus is one embodiment of this: the procurement-and-margin engine.

01
The pressure

Procurement eats the morning, and margin is something you read after the fact.

↓
What we ship first

Fuel: procurement, price & margin

Procurement and price run as one commercial loop — forecast the need, choose supplier and moment, account for the full landed cost of a batch, set price-at-pump by market and demand. Focus: fewer stockouts and surpluses, less overpay, better margin, faster commercial calls.

02
The pressure

Batches go dark in transit; railcars sit, fines accrue, dispatch is manual.

↓
What we ship first

Logistics & fuel supply

Fuel supply made manageable end-to-end — batch movement, delay risk, acceptance discrepancies, railcars and demurrage, depot operations, station delivery, trip execution. Focus: fewer losses, less idle time and fines, less manual dispatch.

03
The pressure

The network number hides where a station leaks money.

↓
What we ship first

Stations, stores & cafés

The station run as a profit point, not only an operational object — underperformance, queues and lost traffic, dispenser and POS violations, shift work, equipment, assortment, write-offs, store conversion. Focus: fewer operational losses, higher station revenue, better customer experience.

04
The pressure

The customer base and B2B portfolio drift, and discounts leak margin.

↓
What we ship first

Customers, loyalty & B2B

The customer base and B2B portfolio turned into a managed source of growth — customer value, churn, frequency and check, promo, reviews, retail media, corporate-client profitability. Focus: higher retention, more margin per customer, fewer wasted discounts, a stronger loyalty program.

05
The pressure

Deviations surface at the month-end close, once they are already a problem.

↓
What we ship first

Finance, money & control

Finance sees the business as a live picture and finds the cause of a deviation faster — margin, costs, cash, receivables, payment risks, financial losses and policy violations become visible before they become a problem. Focus: faster management decisions, fewer leaks, better cash control, trust in the numbers.

06
The pressure

Back-office routine piles up, and violations show up only at the audit.

↓
What we ship first

Supporting functions

The routine taken out and control strengthened across the functions that support the business — HR, legal, MTO procurement, contractors, internal control, documents and back-office. Focus: less manual work, faster processes, clearer spend, and violations visible before the audit, not after.

07
The pressure

Every department runs its own numbers, and AI gets bolted on point by point.

↓
What we ship first

Data & processes — the foundation

The foundation analytics, automation and AI run on — one data base, quality and availability, reporting, knowledge search, office AI tools, process re-engineering and agentic solutions. Focus: one version of truth, less manual work, and AI that scales systemically, not point by point.
