For independent fuel networks.

Your network already has the data — ERP, POS, loyalty, GPS, depots, BI, spreadsheets. What is usually missing is connectedness: the network runs on people, calls, manual reconciliation and the experience of individual managers, while the data you have piled up does not yet work for your decisions.

How the path runs

AI earns its place on top of a foundation — so the path runs bottom-up.

AI agents are not installed on top of chaos. Every block of the network economy climbs the same way — start small on data you already have, build the foundation as you go. You do not need to be at the top to begin.

  1. Stage 1

    Essential solutions

    Quick to launch on the data you already have. A clear effect without a big process change.

    →
  2. Stage 2

    Advanced solutions

    Need more mature data, integrations and models. A bigger effect from optimizing decisions and processes.

    →
  3. Stage 3

    Agentic & systemic solutions

    End-to-end solutions and AI agents that run on top of the foundation. The person sets the rules and controls the result.

Engagement starting points

Across the whole network economy.

The effect runs across the whole network economy — procurement and margin, pricing, logistics, stations, finance and control. We start where the diagnostic points.

Fuel: procurement, price & margin

Procurement and price run as one commercial loop — forecast the need, choose supplier and moment, account for the full landed cost of a batch, set price-at-pump by market and demand.

  • Buy fuel in the right volume at the right moment
  • Choose the supplier and purchase terms
  • Account for the full landed cost of a batch
  • Set price by market and demand

Logistics & fuel supply

Fuel supply made manageable end-to-end — batch movement, delay risk, acceptance discrepancies, railcars and demurrage, depot operations, station delivery, trip execution.

  • Track batch movement and delay risk
  • Find where you lose on supply discrepancies
  • Control railcars and demurrage
  • Plan fuel delivery to stations

Stations, stores & cafés

The station run as a profit point, not only an operational object — underperformance, queues and lost traffic, dispenser and POS violations, shift work, equipment, assortment, store conversion.

  • Understand why a station underperforms
  • Stop losses at the dispenser and the till
  • Keep station equipment in service
  • Turn fuel traffic into store purchases

Customers, loyalty & B2B

The customer base and B2B portfolio turned into a managed source of growth — customer value, churn, frequency and check, promo, reviews, retail media, corporate-client profitability.

  • Understand the customer and their economics
  • Retain customers and prevent churn
  • Grow frequency, check and non-fuel share
  • Run and measure promotions

Finance, money & control

Finance sees the business as a live picture and finds the cause of a deviation faster — margin, costs, cash, receivables, payment risks, financial losses and policy violations made visible early.

  • See the financial picture here and now
  • Understand the cause of a deviation, not just the fact
  • Anticipate cash and cash-flow gaps
  • Surface financial losses and discrepancies

Supporting functions

The routine taken out and control strengthened across the functions that support the business — HR, legal, MTO procurement, contractors, internal control, documents and back-office.

  • HR and workforce
  • Legal
  • MTO procurement and contractors
  • Internal control, losses and risk

Data & processes — the foundation

The foundation analytics, automation and AI run on — one data base, quality and availability, reporting, knowledge search, office AI tools, process re-engineering and agentic solutions.

  • One source of truth instead of different numbers per department
  • Find answers in the company’s knowledge
  • Take routine off the team
