Industries — Fuel retail

For independent fuel networks.

Your network already has the data — ERP, POS, loyalty, GPS, depots, BI, spreadsheets. What is usually missing is connectedness: the network runs on people, calls, manual reconciliation and the experience of individual managers, while the data you have piled up does not yet work for your decisions.

How the path runs

AI earns its place on top of a foundation — so the path runs bottom-up.

AI agents are not installed on top of chaos. Every block of the network economy climbs the same way — start small on data you already have, build the foundation as you go. You do not need to be at the top to begin.

  1. Essential

    See & control, now

    Bring the picture together on data you already have — need, stock, supplier offers, your price against the market. Cheap, fast, no models.

    →
  2. Advanced

    Forecast & optimize

    Forecast demand, find the right volume and moment, price by local sensitivity. Builds on the foundation beneath it.

    →
  3. Frontier

    Decisions under AI

    Agents prepare the order or hold price sharp within your rules — the person sets the bounds and approves. Never AI on its own.

Engagement starting points

Across the whole network economy.

The effect runs across the whole network economy — procurement and margin, pricing, logistics, stations, finance and control. We start where the diagnostic points. Optimus is one embodiment of this: the procurement-and-margin engine, in pilot at Red Petroleum.

Fuel: procurement, price & margin

Procurement eats the morning, and margin is something you read after the fact.

Procurement and price run as one commercial loop — forecast the need, choose supplier and moment, account for the full landed cost of a batch, set price-at-pump by market and demand. Fewer stockouts and surpluses, less overpay, better margin, faster commercial calls.

Logistics & fuel supply

Batches go dark in transit; railcars sit, fines accrue, dispatch is manual.

Fuel supply made manageable end-to-end — batch movement, delay risk, acceptance discrepancies, railcars and demurrage, depot operations, station delivery, trip execution. Fewer losses, less idle time and fines, less manual dispatch.

Stations, stores & cafés

The network number hides where a station leaks money.

The station run as a profit point, not only an operational object — underperformance, queues and lost traffic, dispenser and POS violations, shift work, equipment, assortment, write-offs, store conversion. Fewer operational losses, higher station revenue, better customer experience.

Customers, loyalty & B2B

The customer base and B2B portfolio drift, and discounts leak margin.

The customer base and B2B portfolio turned into a managed source of growth — customer value, churn, frequency and check, promo, reviews, retail media, corporate-client profitability. Higher retention, more margin per customer, fewer wasted discounts, a stronger loyalty program.

Finance, money & control

Deviations surface at the month-end close, once they are already a problem.

Finance sees the business as a live picture and finds the cause of a deviation faster — margin, costs, cash, receivables, payment risks, financial losses and policy violations become visible before they become a problem. Faster decisions, fewer leaks, better cash control, trust in the numbers.

Supporting functions

Back-office routine piles up, and violations show up only at the audit.

The routine taken out and control strengthened across the functions that support the business — HR, legal, MTO procurement, contractors, internal control, documents and back-office. Less manual work, faster processes, clearer spend, violations visible before the audit.

Data & processes — the foundation

Every department runs its own numbers, and AI gets bolted on point by point.

The foundation analytics, automation and AI run on — one data base, quality and availability, reporting, knowledge search, office AI tools, process re-engineering and agentic solutions. One version of truth, less manual work, AI that scales systemically.
