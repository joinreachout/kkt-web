Honest fit

When we are the right partner — and when we are not.

We would rather tell you early that we are not the best fit than sell you an engagement that will not land. Here is how to tell — and where to look if it is not us yet.

01

If you are earlier than ~$50M in revenue.

We are built for mid-sized companies — deepest in retail and fuel. Below that scale, our delivery model usually costs more than the value it can create for you — you would be paying for rigor you do not need yet. There are excellent partners sized for smaller companies, and starting with one of them is the right first move.

02

If you want strategy without anyone shipping it.

Our work is shaped to put something operating into your business, not to produce a deck that sits on a shelf. If what you need right now is a slide-led strategy with no delivery team behind it, a strategy house will serve you better than we would.

03

If there is no senior owner inside to inherit the work.

The systems we build keep running after we leave only if someone senior inside owns them — with real authority and real time, not just a title. If that person is not in place yet, the honest first step is to get them in seat, and we are glad to help you scope the role before we would start.

04

If you need a model in production in four weeks.

We can run a diagnostic in four weeks. A model live in your business and moving real numbers in four weeks — that we cannot promise, and we would rather say so than over-commit. Good delivery has a floor on time, and we would happily walk you through a realistic one.

We would rather quote you a timeline that holds than one that sounds good in the room.

05

If you are in heavily regulated banking, public sector, or defence.

These domains carry compliance bars and delivery patterns we do not claim deep expertise in. We respect that work enough not to pretend — firms built specifically for it will serve you better, and that is who we would point you toward.

06

If the goal is just to “deploy some AI”.

If the ask is to deploy a chatbot, roll out Copilot, or run an LLM pilot with no business outcome attached, we are probably not your best call — we put data, models, and AI to work only where they earn their place against margin, cash, availability, customer value, or productivity. If there is a real outcome underneath the ask, though, let us talk about that instead.

07

If you need a team to run things for you.

We work alongside your people and bring senior delivery rigor, AI capability, and PMO discipline — but we do not take over your operations, your IT department, or your data function. If what you need is full-service outsourcing or staff augmentation, a different kind of firm is the better fit.

The other half of this rigor — how we treat numbers when we do quote them — lives on /methods/cost-of-doing-nothing. Public, dated, sourced.

If you read all of this and still want to talk, that is usually a good sign.
