Case study — Ride-hailing · Engagement in progress (client unnamed until consent)

A Baltic ride-hailing platform — pricing and demand, learned from the data.

The approach
  • Data deep-dive on growth — not a dashboard refresh. Each business question (low daily rides, long wait times, riders not returning, onboarding drop-off) tested against hypotheses, down to an actionable recommendation.
  • Dynamic pricing — real-time fare adjustment to demand, traffic, time of day, and events. The one place we'd reach past traditional ML toward a time-series model where it earns its keep.
  • Ride-demand forecasting — predict demand by area and time so drivers are positioned ahead of it, cutting rider wait and lifting driver utilisation.
  • Driver-performance signals — ratings, punctuality, cancellations modelled to surface who to reward and where support or training lands.
  • LLM support assistant — an off-the-shelf assistant fine-tuned for onboarding and the common rider and driver questions, with sentiment analysis on feedback.

Client unnamed while the engagement is in progress — named once the consent tier allows, or not mentioned at all. No invented outcomes: this is the work as it actually runs.
